# Android games and apps

Foldcade automatically finds installed apps with launcher icons. Games start in **Android Games**, other apps in **Android Apps**, and recognized emulators and streaming apps in **Emulators**.

## Add a game

1. Install the game and return to Foldcade.
2. Find it in **Android Games**, **Android Apps**, or **All**. The L1 drawer also opens the Games and Apps lists.
3. Press **A** to open it on the top screen or **X** on the bottom.

Android decides whether an app is a game. Use **Y › Move to Android Games/Apps** from its Home folder or L1 shelf to change its classification.

Empty default folders are hidden. If Android Games is absent, check Android Apps and All.

## Arrange your apps

Press **Y** to move tiles, add them to collections, or **Hide** them. Hidden tiles stay in All; **Show** in **L1 › Edit home › Hidden** restores their former slots, pins, and collections without reinstalling the app. The Android shelves share this hidden state.

Check [New games on Home](README.md#a-game-is-in-all-but-missing-from-home) if new apps appear only in All.

An emulator's tile opens the app. Add its games through [local folders](local-games.md), [RomM](romm.md), or the relevant [source guide](README.md).

## PlayStation streaming with PXPlay

PXPlay (PS Play, `psplay.grill.com`) streams your PS5 or PS4. It opens on the top screen and takes both screens: while it streams, its DualSense touchpad and buttons are on the bottom screen, and Foldcade leaves that screen alone instead of dimming it. Press **A** to play it. To change what goes there, open PXPlay's settings and set **Content on external display** to **Gamepad overlay** or **Stream view**.

PXPlay has no links into a console or a game, so its tile opens PXPlay's console list. To go straight into a stream, turn on **Auto-connect on app launch** in your console's settings in PXPlay.

## Updates and saves

The list updates at startup, when you return, and when apps are installed, removed, or changed. Apps without launcher icons do not become tiles.

Each app handles its own saves and cloud sync; Foldcade does not back up Android saves to RomM.
